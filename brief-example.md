## Snapshot

Tavily builds a search‑layer API that lets AI agents access real‑time, verified web information for Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) applications. Its platform provides high‑performance web search, crawling and structured data extraction so that large language models can incorporate up‑to‑date external knowledge, reduce hallucinations and comply with enterprise governance policies. The service is aimed at developers and businesses creating AI agents, offering a usage‑based API that integrates public and private sources into LLM‑driven workflows.

## Key people

Tavily's leadership team is currently led by its Founder and CEO, Rotem Weiss. The company was co-founded by Rotem Weiss, Assaf Elovic, and Yuval Rozio, who served as Managing Director at Alpha Wave Global. Tavily has recently been acquired by Nebius Group NV, and while continuing to operate under the Tavily brand, Rotem Weiss and his team will join Nebius. The acquisition was reported to be for $275 million, as per Bloomberg. Tavily has demonstrated significant market traction with over 3 million monthly SDK downloads and serves clients including IBM, Cohere, and Groq.

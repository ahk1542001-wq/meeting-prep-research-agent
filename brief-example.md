## Snapshot

Tavily is a real‑time search engine for AI agents and RAG workflows, providing fast and secure APIs for web search and content extraction that let agents search, crawl, extract and reason with live web data. The platform delivers a single, high‑performance search layer that combines web‑page extraction and intelligent crawling, enabling enterprises and Fortune 500 companies to connect agents to public and private sources, reduce hallucinations and bias, and build more reliable AI systems.

## Key people

Tavily's current Founder and CEO is Rotem Weiss, who has a background in GPT research and has worked with Talenya, the Israeli Ministry of Defense, and the Israel Immigration Absorption Ministry. Tavily has 14 executives, and the company has been acquired by Nebius Group NV for $275 million, with Rotem Weiss and his team joining Nebius while continuing to operate under the Tavily brand.

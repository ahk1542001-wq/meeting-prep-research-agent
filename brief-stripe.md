## Snapshot

Stripe is a fintech and software‑as‑a‑service company that builds economic infrastructure for the internet, enabling businesses of all sizes to accept online and mobile payments and manage their operations. It provides payment‑processing software and APIs that let merchants take debit and credit‑card payments, create payment links, and handle billing, invoicing, subscription and usage‑based billing, as well as tools such as Atlas, Connect, Radar (fraud prevention), Issuing, Terminal, Capital, Checkout, Climate, Corporate Card, Data Pipeline, Elements, Financial Connections, Identity and more. Stripe also offers support services, a professional‑services marketplace, and a partner ecosystem, and works with financial institutions, regulators, payment networks, banks and consumer wallets so companies can focus on their products rather than payment infrastructure.

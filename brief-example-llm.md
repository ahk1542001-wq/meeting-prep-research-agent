## Snapshot

Tavily is a “web access layer for AI agents” that offers real‑time search, content extraction, site mapping, crawling and cited research through five REST‑based APIs (Search, Extract, Map, Crawl and Research) delivering clean, LLM‑ready text instead of raw HTML. Its platform is built to give large language models up‑to‑date, verifiable information and reduce hallucinations, supporting Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) use cases for enterprises such as IBM, Cohere, Groq, MongoDB and Writer. Founded by Rotem Weiss (CEO) and Assaf Elovic, the company was acquired by Nebius in early 2026 and operates under its own brand, offering a free tier of 1,000 monthly credits and paid plans starting at $30 per month.

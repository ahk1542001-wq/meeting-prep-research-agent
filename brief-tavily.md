## Snapshot

Tavily is a privately held B2B SaaS company founded in 2024 that builds a real‑time search engine and web‑access layer for AI agents and Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) workflows. Its suite of developer‑friendly APIs lets enterprises and developers search, crawl, and extract structured insights from both public and private web sources, providing high‑performance, compliant, low‑latency web access that reduces hallucinations and bias. The platform is positioned as the “search layer” that connects AI agents to the live internet, and it is already used by companies such as Groq, Cohere, MongoDB and Writer to power secure, reliable, and scalable agentic search.
